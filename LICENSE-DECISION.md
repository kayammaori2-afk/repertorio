# License decision

On 2026-11-16 the repository owner authorized choosing a permissive license and publishing Repertorio v0.1.0. The project uses **MIT** for its code, documentation, original catalog, synthetic benchmark and artificial pilot fixtures. One license keeps reuse straightforward across the distributed package. Preserve the copyright and license notice when redistributing covered material. The governing text is [LICENSE](LICENSE); see [GitHub's MIT summary](https://choosealicense.com/licenses/mit/).

This supersedes the earlier proposal of separate MIT/CC BY terms and historical "pending owner decision" notes. Review transcripts and prior frozen manifests remain as dated evidence, not current policy. The publication freeze updates only catalog rights metadata, without changing mappings, weights, labels or primary rankings.

Dependencies retain their own licenses. They are installed separately rather than vendored into the Repertorio wheel; [the inventory](evidence/dependency-licenses.json) records their metadata and is not a legal certification. No copied scenes, books, scripts, covers or real learner profiles are bundled as fixtures. Rights in user-supplied profiles remain with their respective holders; exporting a profile does not publish it or relicense it under MIT.
