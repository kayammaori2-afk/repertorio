The focused final reports `review-deletion-final.md` and `review-methodology-closeout.md` closed the material repair findings in their examined scope. They did not run tests or certify the entire product. The final local test and clean-install evidence supplies that separate execution record.

Material corrections include removal of deleted payloads from retry/preview tables, temporary default demo storage, accurate client-reported delivery provenance, integer revision bounds, normalized legacy migrations, robust private-file Windows handling, read scope required alongside mutation permissions, and deletion of linked sessions for current catalog references and declared historical links. The final focused review in `evidence/review-deletion-final.md` closed the indirect-link finding under that documented boundary. Deterministic retired request-ID hashes remain to block stale replay. Their guess-confirmation limitation is explicit in docs/data.md; this is not physical erasure.

Methodology corrections preserve original rankings and the negative held-out result. They expose shared-author label bias, asserted rather than independently timestamped preregistration, tied baselines, dependent variants, standard recall ceilings and the limits of the safety oracle. Pilot items were rewritten before any recruitment to avoid the identified numerical/context overlap; the revised bank has finite automated overlap checks and still needs subject-matter and measurement review. Bounds precede assumption-dependent complete-case estimates. The final `freeze-v3-post-followup` also records symmetric tie diagnostics and a clarified scoring rubric. Prior manifests and v1 outputs remain available. Frozen JSON data use byte-preserving Git attributes so checkout line-ending conversion cannot invalidate their hashes.

## Results and limits

The selector's overall C−B nDCG@3 difference is +0.04673529 across 25 applicable constructed cases, but **the designated held-out concept split is −0.05030911 across 12 cases under the fixed ID tie-break**. Symmetric post-result random-tie diagnostics give +0.15140236 there: even the sign depends on tie order. Both are descriptive and neither repairs shared-author label bias. The 25 cases contain 13 distinct objective/eligible-set/grade setups, with dependent variants. No weights were tuned to reverse the primary result. No human benefit, general superiority or LLM tutor-quality improvement follows from it. See the regenerable report and diagnostics in `evidence/evaluation/`.

The advisory scan found no known vulnerabilities in the checked third-party versions at execution time; the unpublished local project is explicitly skipped by that database. The license inventory records installed package metadata rather than a legal certification. The source/history scan detects common secret patterns and private-state filenames; it cannot prove absence of every possible secret. The publishing artifact contents were also inspected.

Not executed: Linux/macOS runtime, GitHub-hosted CI, remote ChatGPT connection, public deployment, independent human annotation, expert pedagogical review, participant pilot or real-world adoption study. At the time of this initial record, licensing and publication were pending and no public push/tag had occurred. The later publication authorization and current release evidence are in publication.md. No package-registry upload, account creation or tunnel is part of the release.
