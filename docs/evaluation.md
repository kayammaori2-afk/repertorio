# Internal selector evaluation

This release evaluates context selection, not tutor behavior or student learning. The production selector is deterministic and needs no LLM, embedding service or network. All 16 mappings and 31 cases are original hypothetical examples. They do not reproduce any film, book, scene or real person's history. The cooperative-room story is an original miniature story with an explicitly versioned edition and spoiler position. Catalog review status is internal authorship, not subject-matter validation.

## Preregistered design and provenance

`src/repertorio/data/preregistration.json` contains the author’s assertion that it was written before scoring code and evaluation output. This timing is not independently timestamped or demonstrated by the initial commit. The policy weights were fixed a priori (4 × structural mapping strength, 2 × confirmed familiarity, 3 × exact objective-tag overlap, 1 × previously reported use, zero affinity weight). No optimizer or result-based adjustment was used. Familiarity is not interest. Reported historical use is a low-weight self/tutor report, not proof of verified use or learning. Delivery alone adds no weight. Concept-specific tags and author-assigned structural strengths are deliberately small heuristics, not calibrated probabilities.

The implementation agent authored catalog, cases, relevance grades and rationales. The catalog’s structural judgments existed before case grades and informed them. Grades were not arithmetically calculated from the score, but they are not independent of its principal input; observed agreement can be close to circular. This is **internal synthetic evidence with shared-author bias**, even though the grading code is independent of the ranking score. No independent annotation, tutor evaluation, human experiment or external validation took place. Explicit baseline-strength cases favor relevant exceptions and limitations, while generic structural mappings can score higher under the fixed policy. Negative results must remain visible.

Development concept groups are opportunity cost and independence. Held-out test groups are marginal analysis and conditional probability, plus an outside-catalog group. Variants within a concept are dependent and do not count as independent experiments. The author reports the groups were fixed before results; there was no learning or weight fitting on development cases. This is a grouped descriptive comparison, not learned transfer across concepts. Therefore “test” denotes the designated split, not evidence of an independently sourced dataset. The tiny purposive sample supports descriptive comparisons only; no population confidence interval is reported.

`frozen_manifest.json` hashes selector code, evaluator code, catalog, cases and preregistration before the final evaluation. Evaluation refuses changed inputs. Freeze metadata records any corrections made before the final measured run; policy changes after seeing results require a new policy and manifest version, with the reason recorded. Formatting changes after a freeze also require an explicit new freeze. Environment and measured latency are separated from deterministic semantic artifacts.

## Arms and measurement

All arms receive the same concept, objective, academic evidence and 12,000-byte maximum. A receives no repertoire and has no applicable ranking metric. B takes up to five declared favorites, ordered by affinity then stable item ID, from all catalog-supported experiences passing the same hard filters; it supplies at most three concept-matching catalog mappings from those favorites, retaining affinity order. This makes the baseline stronger than titles alone: it receives the same curated explanations where available, but no full score or gold labels. The five-favorite cap can omit a relevant lower-affinity experience.

C calls production `prepare_context` with k=3. B/C jointly change availability through the favorite cap and ordering, and the context can differ in length within the equal maximum budget. They do not isolate the ranker. D isolates ranking: exactly C's eligible candidates and k=3, ordered by exact tag overlap then stable reference ID. All ranking policies use the same hard safety gates. The academic package is copied unchanged to every arm. A fallback still supports conventional teaching; A's deliberate absence of repertoire is not scored as an incorrect abstention.

The primary metric is nDCG@3 with gain `2^grade−1` and `log2(rank+1)` discount (ranks start at one). Grades range 0–3. The ideal ranking contains all oracle-eligible labeled references for that case, including candidates omitted by favorites. Missing ranks contribute zero; an empty ranking is zero if an eligible positive-grade answer existed. If none existed, nDCG is null and omitted from the mean, while the case remains in coverage, abstention and constraint denominators. Every unlabeled or grade-zero returned reference contributes zero gain. B and D may beat C; those case IDs and paired differences are exported. All returned references receive a duplicate-policy consistency check which does not call the production filter; this is not independent safety evidence. Explicit `forbidden_reference_ids`, manually assigned to exclusion, unknown-familiarity, spoiler, progress, edition and empty-profile scenarios, are also gated independently of the eligibility calculations.

Report denominators, ordinary recall over all eligible positive references (with its per-case top-3 ceiling `min(3, positive_count)/positive_count`), cases with delivered context, correct and unwarranted abstention, constraint violations, and actual serialized UTF-8 bytes. `pairs.json` contains C−B and C−D for every applicable case. `summary.json` includes concept, scenario and split cuts. One local sequential warm-process pass times all four arms together per case; this is not a benchmark of individual arm latency or network/tutor latency. Timings exclude output writes and are in a separate file because they are nondeterministic.

## Reproduce

```console
repertorio evaluate --output evidence/evaluation
repertorio evaluate --output evidence/evaluation-repeat
```

Compare `results.json`, `summary.json`, `pairs.json`, `contexts.json`, `results.csv`, `manifest.json`, `diagnostics.json` and `REPORT.md` byte-for-byte. The test suite performs this check. Do not demand byte equality from `latency.json`; environment records may change across operating systems or Python versions. The frozen input hashes are portable across installed wheels because they refer to bundled package bytes, not repository paths. Code/version provenance for the entire release belongs to the release manifest. An offline replay is never described as an LLM evaluation.

Acceptance means zero observed hard-constraint violations, bounded context, correct fallback and correction behavior, reproducible outputs, and honest reporting. It does **not** require C to win. The report written by the command is the authoritative measured result. The root release records the clean-install invocation and output paths.

## Measured v1 result (2026-11-16)

Across 31 constructed cases, 25 had eligible positive gold and six required abstention. Mean nDCG@3 was B 0.82746, C 0.87420 and D 0.75541; A is not applicable. The overall paired C−B difference was +0.04674, but the held-out concept split was **negative: −0.05031 across 12 applicable cases**. Held-out C−D was +0.01066. B beat C in nine cases, including all four baseline-strength cases and several conditional-probability cases. D beat C in five. These mixed results do not support a general claim that the structured policy is superior.

All four arms had zero observed hard-constraint violations. B/C/D each abstained correctly in six cases and never abstained when a positive eligible reference existed. The maximum observed context was 4,486 UTF-8 bytes. Two executions produced byte-identical semantic artifacts; timings were excluded. The final report, case pairs and frozen hashes are in `evidence/evaluation/`. No weights or grades were adjusted after viewing these results.

One instructive failure is conditional probability: the full policy favors its generic mapping strength while the card example's restricted denominator has a higher task-specific grade than some references ranked above it. A separate baseline-strength task specifically asks about prediction limits, favoring the weather example despite its lower generic score. An expert review and new independently labeled set should precede any policy revision; these examples are not a reason to silently retune the current test.

## Product limits and next test

Only four exact concept IDs are supported: `opportunity_cost`, `marginal_analysis`, `conditional_probability`, `independence`. The assistant interprets the lesson and supplies an ID. Unknown concepts fall back instead of pretending to understand. The selector does not parse arbitrary prose for mastery, infer knowledge from liking an item, fetch URLs, or include imported titles/reviews in pedagogical instructions. It emits curated catalog prose plus minimal counts and structured reasons. Confirmed experience elements are hard prerequisites for the example. Academic prerequisites are listed for a diagnostic check, not inferred from a single success record.

Unknown familiarity, unknown story progress, unknown spoiler limit, mismatched edition and exclusions conservatively reject references. A single missing familiarity declaration can prompt one targeted clarification; otherwise the tutor should proceed directly. Corrections are read from the current profile on every call and never overwritten by catalog assumptions. Scores may miss an objective's nuance; baseline-strength cases expose that limitation. After independent subject-matter review, the next evidence would be a controlled tutor-behavior comparison with fixed models/prompts, then the adult feasibility protocol in pilot.md. Neither is needed to run the technical release, and neither has been claimed as completed.

## Post-result review revision v2

The original frozen hashes are retained in `data/freeze_history_v1.json`. The v2 freeze openly records changes made **after** viewing v1 results: reporting diagnostics, explicit safety expectations and provenance wording. Original score weights, catalog, affinities, objectives and grades remain unchanged, so primary rankings and their negative held-out contrast are preserved. This is not a new preregistration, independent test or repaired external validity.

`diagnostics.json` reports per-case Spearman rank correlations (average ranks for ties) between catalog fit and relevance grades, exact fit/grade matches, all-tie affinity/tag rates and 200 seeded random tie orders per applicable case. Tie priorities are generated without consulting grades; the original planted baseline-strength affinities remain visible. This post-result sensitivity analysis does not replace the fixed-order main benchmark. B’s cap and ordering are often determined by item IDs because affinities tie. D is simply reference-ID order whenever all tag overlaps are zero. The four baseline-strength affinities deliberately favor the objective’s strongest reference and are not an independent preference sample. Future work needs preferences and labels from independent sources, with scorer inputs hidden.

The report leads with the designated test split, equal-weight concept-group contrast, and number of distinct (objective, eligible set, grades) setups rather than treating dependent variants as independent trials. Prior-use history is empty throughout the benchmark; familiarity varies only in correction/unknown cases. Separate unit regressions prove those score components can change a ranking, but provide no effectiveness estimate. Non-baseline wins mainly demonstrate consistency of the shared author’s judgments.


Follow-up revision v3 retains the same primary stable-ID ranking. Tie sensitivity now randomizes equal full-policy scores for C as well as ties in B/D, and reports B/C/D means by split. The observed contrast sign under this sensitivity rule is reported from executed results, rather than inferred from a reviewer’s calculation. Fit/grade correlation is also separated into baseline-strength versus other scenarios and concept groups, alongside each group’s fixed-order C−B. Aggregate correlation must not hide the deliberate countercases.


Publication freeze v4 changes only the catalog rights metadata to MIT after owner authorization. The former v3 manifest is preserved, and numerical rankings are compared to the pre-publication outputs. No scores, labels or pedagogical mappings changed.
